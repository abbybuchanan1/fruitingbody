# Process — working draft

Each series begins with thematic intention rather than a pre-composed image. I set the camera on a tripod and may bring symbolic materials - fruit, honey, pearls, fabric, masks - when they belong to the inquiry. I enter the frame, embody the theme or enact a ritual gesture, step out to reset the camera, and return.

With rare exceptions, nothing is staged in advance; the frame is set, then the body improvises the ritual. The process moves between immersion and interruption. The camera functions as witness.

Most photographs are minimally edited and remain close to what occurred in-camera. When accident aligns with concept, I preserve it.
