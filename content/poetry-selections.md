# Reading Room — version recommendations

These are curatorial version choices, not edits to the poems.

## For C
Use the full/original Reading Room PDF: `For C (1).pdf`.

The recursive recognition structure is part of the poem's intelligence. The longer version earns its scale by moving repeatedly between the mythic and the ordinary; compressing it risks turning the recognition spiral into a list of images rather than an enacted way of thinking.

## May, 2024
Use `May, 2024 (v 2).pdf`.

The revised version preserves the poem's necessary abundance while tightening repetition and transitions. The poem should remain long; its accumulating scale is part of its argument.

## That It Was
Use the full version: `That it was.pdf`.

The repetition of "That it was / That it was not" is structural rather than ornamental. The full version allows care, dread, ordinary detail, and the counterfactual death to accrue enough weight for the form to do its ethical work.
