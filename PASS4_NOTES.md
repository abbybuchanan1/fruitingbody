# Fruiting Body — Pass 4 circulation adjustment

## What changed

The Vestibule now exposes five physically plausible passages:

1. Toward grasses → Relative
2. Toward textiles → Red Room
3. Through the arch → Membrane (primary / authored entrance)
4. Selected Works
5. Toward water → Body of Water

This follows the architectural walkthrough in which the visitor later realizes that the entry vestibule contains multiple corridors rather than a single mandatory route.

## Why

Pass 3 made Membrane the only practical entrance from the Vestibule. That preserved the authored sequence but made Relative and Body of Water unnecessarily dependent on the Map.

Pass 4 separates two ideas:

- **Guided emphasis:** Membrane remains the clearest invitation inward.
- **Architectural adjacency:** other spaces that could physically be reached from the Vestibule are also available.

The visitor can therefore wander without the interface presenting five equal navigation buttons.

## What is intentionally unchanged

- Relative still exits to Vestibule, Narthex, and Back Corridor.
- Body of Water still exits to Vestibule, Narthex, and Cloister/Courtyard.
- Red Room remains one continuous exhibition environment for Red Thread, Shelter, and Unravel.
- Map remains a recovery/orientation layer rather than a required route-finding mechanism.

## Evaluation question

From the Vestibule, can a visitor sense that the museum is larger than the central route without feeling confronted by a menu?
